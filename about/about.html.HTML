About me

Founder of andyuiuxdesign

I live in California’s South Bay. I was first introduced to graphic design during my sophomore year of high school, alongside my fine arts studies. After graduating, I studied at De Anza College, where I took courses in art, design, and photography.

After earning certificates in design and photography, I attended both paid and unpaid workshops to reinforce what I had learned and apply it to my personal work. I don’t hold a Bachelor of Fine Arts, but I have developed a strong sense of what makes good design.
