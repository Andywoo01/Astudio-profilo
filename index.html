A little about me

Design is a practice in paying attention.

I’m Andy, a designer based in California’s South Bay Area. I care about making useful, thoughtful experiences—and bringing a strong visual point of view to every project.

My first introduction to graphic design came during my sophomore year of high school, alongside my studies in fine arts. After graduating, I attended De Anza College, where I took courses in art, design, and photography.

After earning certificates in design and photography, I continued learning through paid and unpaid workshops. I use those experiences in my personal work and keep building on what I’ve learned. I don’t hold a Bachelor of Fine Arts, but I’ve developed a strong sense of what makes design thoughtful and effective.
